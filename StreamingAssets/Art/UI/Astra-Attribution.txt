MANIFOLD TCG — ORIGINAL ASTRA INTERFACE GRAPHICS
Designed for the Unity client, October 2026.

These images were created specifically for Manifold's UI. They contain original
procedural drawing, no third-party assets, no licensed fonts, and no card art.
They can be edited and shipped as part of the Manifold client.

AstraConsoleSurface.png — subtle dark-metal fill, intentionally translucent.
AstraConsoleFrame.png — restrained celestial-bronze frame; 28 px nine-slice.
AstraOptionPlate.png — bevel and transparent steel surface at 384 x 48;
                       12 px nine-slice for 40–48 px UI controls.
AstraOptionHover.png — bright edge-only plate, shown on pointer hover.
AstraOptionSigil.png — separate, aspect-preserving button emblem.
AstraDivider.png — 384 x 18 thin horizontal decision ornament.
AstraHealthRing.png — simplified metallic health indicator artwork.

Credits: original procedurally authored interface graphics for Manifold TCG.
All proprietary art, playmat images, card art and logos remain unchanged.

October 2026 — round and game-log visual refresh:
AstraRoundPlate.png — original opaque charcoal/bronze round-counter skin,
                      44 px nine-sliced for the existing animated round plaque.
AstraLogSurface.png — original dark-metal grain, with faint decorative geometry.
                      (2026-10-31, Brad: its circle painted out; the ruled lines and the two
                      upright lines stay.)
AstraLogFrame.png — original low-contrast gold/cyan thin frame,
                    24 px nine-sliced and drawn behind the log text.
These are generated interface shapes and textures, not replacements for existing
Rounds/RoundN.png, playmats, dice, or card artwork. The round plaque skin is
an overlay added at runtime by Ui.cs; all source images remain untouched.

October 2026 — instance-aware option artwork and shared menu styling:
AstraCardGlyph.png — original 64x64 bronze/cyan generic card symbol.
AstraDiceGlyph.png — original 64x64 bronze/cyan generic dice symbol.

These two original fallback symbols are used only when a decision does not
identify an exact card printing or dice face visible in the current GameView.
Known cards and dice use their original StreamingAssets artwork instead.
The console, deck builder, setup and main menu now share the same existing
Astra option surfaces, hover edges, and restrained large-window frame.
No card pictures, alternate printing artworks, playmats or game logos are
altered or copied into the UI-assets folder.
