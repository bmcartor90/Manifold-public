The game's music: scores chosen by Brad (October 2026), all by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com).
Each was trimmed of the silence at its start and end, faded briefly at both ends so it loops cleanly, leveled to the
same loudness (-18 LUFS) and converted to Ogg Vorbis for the game; the web tutorial's one (Call to Adventure) to MP3
too, for browsers that can't play Ogg. The game's Settings > About lists the same credits.
Which plays where: Manifold.Engine/Host/MusicTracks.cs.
