MANIFOLD ORIGINAL AUDIO RE-SCORE — 2026-10-26
All 36 <cue>.wav files are newly generated, original procedural sound effects
produced specifically for the Manifold Unity interface. They do not reproduce,
sample, remix or incorporate the original Kenney or Freesound recordings.

The new mono 32,000 Hz / 16-bit PCM WAV cues are short and peak-normalized:
- Interface: click, back, confirm, warning, not-allowed, your-turn, tutorial-note
- Turn: new-round, ready
- Cards: play, play-organic, draw, shuffle, activate, attach
- Dice and locales: roll-one, roll, resolve, locale-made, pay
- Magic and abilities: energy, modify, sabotage, rend, shield-up, shield-break, shock, dig-in
- Impact: damage, damage-mechanical, damage-organic, damage-ethereal, destroyed, hero-defeated
- Outcome: victory, defeat

The existing *.ogg recordings selected earlier remain intact and licensed as
listed above. Sounds.cs loads these originals first, then the optional original
*.wav files; if one is deleted or cannot load the corresponding .ogg still plays.
These files require no third-party attribution. All artwork and sound effects
are confined to the private game repository.
